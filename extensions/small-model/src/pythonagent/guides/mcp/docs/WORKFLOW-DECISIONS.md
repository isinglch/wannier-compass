# Orbital manifolds and windows: canonical decision sequence

This document defines the scientific decision order and the evidence required at each step. See [MANIFOLD-SELECTION.md](MANIFOLD-SELECTION.md) for commands and JSON fields, and [PHYSICAL-REASONING.md](PHYSICAL-REASONING.md) for interpretation. A figure, numerical gate, or explanation establishes only the property it checks; none substitutes for validation of the fitted model.

## Inputs and objectives

Use a consistent SCF energy reference, the complete projected band path, raw element-by-s/p/d/f state weights, occupations, and band indices from the same calculation. First declare the physical states and precision objective that the model must reproduce; then choose its orbital representation and review the complete required manifold, including relevant conduction states. A complete target means the declared physical task, not every chemically filled shell or every state with nonzero projection. There is no fixed `EF±1 eV` orbital-selection interval, and no additional projected DOS or independent NSCF mesh is required before selection. Band indices are energy-ordered at each k point; they are not tracked wavefunction branches.

Record `task_scope_review` before assigning basis or target rows. Identify the task's physical states in `task_anchor_band_ranges_1based`, then mark concentrated deep occupied blocks and their roles. The default `primary_task_manifold` objective covers the main physically relevant orbital manifold and related low conduction; a broader deep-bonding model requires `explicitly_requested_extended_manifold` and the actual request. Defining a deep block as target because an extra orbital was chosen is circular reasoning.

Before finalizing orbitals, inspect the same angular channels in the remaining task valence and relevant low conduction. Main chemical candidates are p for the p block, d for the d block, s for the s block, and available f for the f series. The f-series hint does not establish occupation or task relevance. Additional shell channels are reviewed model hypotheses. Include an extra channel when it has an independent task role or a concrete representation concern worth testing; cite a task-state witness and explain its contribution in `additional_basis_evidence`. A concentrated deep bonding peak, residual mixing, or absence of a global gap alone does not justify inclusion. If the role is unresolved, record an exploratory task component and test the hypothesis within budget. Mathematical impossibility of a smaller model need not be proved before a first trial.

For each channel, record its task role and risks of both inclusion and omission. For an extra channel in a primary-task model, its witness must be outside the declared deep blocks; an explicit extended task may instead request a deep target. Deep blocks mean specifically reviewed concentrated lower blocks, not the whole occupied manifold. Raw nonzero projection validates a witness coordinate only; it is not a numerical inclusion threshold. Basis omission, target membership, optional band exclusion, and lower windows remain separate decisions. Energy-ordered projection continuity does not prove wavefunction connectivity.

Occupation alone does not set the boundary. A conservative `initial` must identify the first unprotected target-conduction state and explain its deferral. Self-declared target rows alone cannot classify a state as unwanted. |

Negative screen | After basis, target, exclusions, outer, and positive frozen candidate are fixed, inspect raw projections at every included path state and numerical gates | `frozen_state_counterexample_v1` reviews only the proposed inner window. ## Criterion priority

1. Input/matrix identity, nested windows, state counts, computed top, and interface-mesh gates are necessary execution conditions; they do not establish physical validity.
2. The independently declared task determines basis scope. Main chemical candidates guide inspection; they do not mandate every bonding shell. Additional channels require task-related evidence. Residual mixing permits a reduced-basis hypothesis, while appreciable task mixing may justify an explicit extension or an exploratory comparison. No universal s ban or fixed EF interval applies.
3. Approximately 70% coverage/tail onset, band edge `+0.5 eV`, rounding, and margins generate candidates; they cannot override the complete target envelope or certify a fit.
4. The negative screen has a restricted scope: an already proposed frozen interval and its corresponding lower/upper boundaries. Record conflicting evidence and trial intent; do not transfer its conclusion to basis or outer selection.

## Valence lower-edge procedure

First mark the lowest target-valence state and retained unwanted/weakly projected lower states in the projected bands. Place `dis_min` below the target minimum with an initial margin; a small amount of background inside outer is acceptable. Independently find `frozen_min` that protects clean target states without freezing lower background. If target/background energies overlap without a scalar separating edge, keep mixed target states in outer and protect only the clearly identifiable interior; record unprotected target states and mixing locations.

For a concentrated deep s block outside a p-derived task, first omit that block from the target and review whether s has a separate necessary role in the remaining task. Keeping some s-mixed DFT states in DIS does not require an explicit s basis channel. Conversely, keeping s for a required low-conduction role does not automatically request all deep s bonding states. Define scope and validate the actual representation in both cases.

Finally, decide separately whether a deep occupied non-target prefix has a safe global gap and can be excluded. Continue window selection and trials when no prefix qualifies. The tool's 0.1 eV path-gap margin applies only to optional `exclude_bands`, not to `dis_min` or `frozen_min`.

## Conduction upper-edge procedure

1. Mark persistent selected-shell conduction contributions and competing shells in the complete projected bands. Declare `target` and the broader `reference` used to inspect tails. At crossings, review figures, energy ranges, and raw state weights; energy-ordered rows are not wavefunction tracking.
2. Generate an initial `dis_max` from the target envelope, `N_outer≥N_wann`, and reference-conditioned 70%/tail evidence. The coverage denominator contains only the reviewed reference rows; it does not measure complete selected-shell coverage.
3. Inspect `upper_reference_audit` in `selection.json`/`review_diagnostics.md` and the complete plots. Review all computed states above outer, inside and outside reference: both branches crossing outer and strong branches starting just above it. For target continuation, revise target/reference, propose a containing candidate, and re-run `propose`. For competing blocks or diffuse tails, document evidence for retaining outer. For ambiguous roles, record uncertainty and test an explicit exploratory candidate. A reference reaching the last computed row, or a probe exceeding the computed top at some path k points, may produce apparent decay through **right censoring**. Obtain sufficient higher-band projections at the same settings before claiming completeness. The audit reports facts and never automatically changes windows or basis.
4. Fix the physically needed outer before auditing DFT sufficiency. Precheck path counts and band-top margin; after interface generation, check the **actual interface mesh**. Report path and interface band tops separately: insufficient path data limits selection evidence; insufficient interface waves limit disentanglement space. Preserve a justified physical candidate, increase NBANDS, and regenerate the required non-self-consistent path and/or shared interface waves. Do not shrink justified `dis` to pass the band-top gate. Retain the candidate with `status=needs_more_data`, `next_action=add_bands_nscf`, and the specific missing source. Recompute checks after adding data. Even a few eV of top margin can warrant review; 5–10 eV is a possible caution scale, not a universal hard threshold or an outer selector. `window_checks` reports minimum/median/maximum per-k `N_outer`, `N_frozen`, `N_outer-num_wann`, and band-top margin. Global NBANDS alone is insufficient: large top margin can coexist with almost no disentanglement headroom. Choose frozen independently from clean low-conduction target evidence, then screen that candidate; outer's high-energy tail does not set frozen.

## Evidence retained for every decision

Preserve `review.json`, `selection.json`, `review_diagnostics.json`, and standard `selection.png/pdf` separately for `initial`, `real_first_try`, and each retry. [Gallery records](SELECTION-GALLERY.md) bind figures to choices by hash; sealed stages and standard selection-only layouts refresh the experiment gallery by default. Other layouts can use the explicit gallery CLI. Record states, candidates, adopted boundaries, reasons, and uncertainty. `record_consistent` checks record/numerical consistency only. After execution, distinguish convergence, complete products, fixed-target fit, and compute/token cost. Use the [retry card](WINDOW-RETRIES.md) to choose the next experiment from the existing protection range. Historical answers remain outside the selection package; replaying development materials is regression evidence.
